# Practice Areas & Fees

Moylan Law LLC is dedicated to providing transparent, high-quality legal services. Value is not just a pricing strategy, but a statement of how the firm interacts with the world—with integrity, honesty, and responsiveness.

### Transactional Services
Flat rates apply for most transactional (non-litigation) matters. Estate planning, Trust and Will drafting, real estate closings, business formations, deeds, advice, and consultation are flat rate.

### Litigation
Litigation is handled on an hourly billing rate, as the uncertain nature of court proceedings makes flat rates impossible.

### Limited Scope Services
Available when a client needs discrete representation, such as document drafting, contract consultation, or managing pre-litigation discovery.

### Real Estate – Commercial and Residential
Constance sets up systems of communication between participants (brokers, lenders, appraisers) to provide a smooth transaction. Services include contract review, tax assessment consultation, oversight of inspections, title clearance, and closing supervision for both residential and commercial properties.
