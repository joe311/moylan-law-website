Moylan Law LLC specializes in Property Law, which ranges from estate planning, trusts, real property, animal law, prenuptial agreements, asset protection, small business services, and succession planning. 

## Legal Services Offered

### Trust & Estate Planning
There is no one-size-fits-all Estate. Constance guides clients through the planning phase to focus on their own needs, from tax abatement to blended family legacies, foreign spouses, or generation-skipping trusts. Your Trust will be custom-drafted for your goals.

### Probate
Constance has successfully guided many families through the Probate process, whether the deceased person left a will or died intestate. This includes navigating disputes, fiduciary accounting, missing funds, and missing heirs.

### Guardianship
Assistance through the difficult process of court guardianship for a family member facing aging issues, dementia, special needs, mental illness, or addictions.

### Real Property and Title Issues
With more than 20 years of experience in the real estate sector, Constance assists clients in a broad spectrum of real estate matters, from investment and acquisition strategies to 1031 exchanges.
