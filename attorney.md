# Constance J. Moylan

From international boardrooms to Navajo Nation peacebuilding, Constance brings a sophisticated, global perspective to the sensitive world of estates and legacy. An Illinois native with a career spanning France, Germany, California, and Colorado, she offers a rare blend of high-level legal ethics and tenacious advocacy for individuals and families.

### Education & Admissions
* **Bar Admissions:** State of Illinois, United States Northern District Illinois
* **J.D., Chicago-Kent College of Law** (Cum Laude)
* **University of Denver Sturm School of Law** (International & Comparative Law)
* **B.A. & Masters, Northwestern University**

### A Foundation in Legal Ethics
Before founding her firm in 2015, Constance honed her expertise in legal ethics at the Attorney Registration and Discipline Commission (ARDC). Her work there ensures that every estate plan and probate filing is rooted in the highest standards of professional integrity.

### Service-Driven Community Roots
Constance dedicates 10% of her practice to giving back to society via pro bono representation. She is a passionate supporter of domestic violence awareness, animal rights, and the arts.
